# Central — state

What central holds that no project file does. The handoff skill rewrites it as a snapshot of now when the
conversation rotates; the next central reads it after the briefing. History lives in git.

## 1. In flight
One line per piece of work started and not yet back: `- what — who has it (session, folder) — since`.

## 2. Promised to the owner
What central told the owner it would do or check, and when.

## 3. Owner's recent answers not yet written elsewhere
Normally empty: an answer goes to the card and the project's STATE.md at once.

## 4. Next
The next action of central, and how it knows it is done.

# Design system

The owner's picks, piece by piece. Screens and videos follow it without asking again; something it does not cover
becomes a new open piece (a "## <Piece>" section with For: and Status: open), and only that piece goes to the owner.

How a piece closes:
1. Its specialist (designer for the visual pieces; tone, rhythm or script for the video ones) runs a fresh web search
   of what the top of the market shipped in the last months, then writes `design/options/<piece>.md` from
   the plugin's `templates/design/options.md`: 2-3 concrete references, each with its exact link, its date and what
   this project takes from it. The library and memory never replace that search.
2. The independent reviewer opens every link and writes `design/reviews/options/<YYYY-MM-DD HHMM> <piece>.md`
   (Options:, Verdict:, Links checked:). An old, weak or broken reference is rejected and replaced before the owner
   sees anything.
3. The coordinator shows the owner the reviewed options, the owner picks, the coordinator writes Status: chosen,
   Options: and Pick: below, then records the owner's OK: `track.sh ok <project folder> design:<piece> <the owner's
   words>`. The OK freezes the piece as written above its "### Applied" part, and its proposal: changing either
   needs a new OK. Nobody else picks; "Status: not applicable" with Why: also needs the owner's OK.
4. Under "### Applied" the specialists write what they derive from the pick (exact values, tokens, rules).

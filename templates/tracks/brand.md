# Track: brand
For creating a brand from design to logo to visual identity, strategy first. Pick it when a new product, company or project needs a name, a mark and rules for using them.

## Stage 1: Strategy
Gate: positioning, audience and personality are written and evidenced; no naming or mark work starts before this.
- [ ] positioning | Positioning statement: target, frame of reference, point of difference, reason to believe | card: marketing | needs: - | evidence:
- [ ] audience | Audience profile with at least 3 primary sources | card: researcher | needs: - | evidence:
- [ ] visual-audit | Competitive visual audit of at least 5 rivals with dated captures: marks, colours, type, conventions and clichés | card: designer | needs: - | evidence:
- [ ] personality | 3 to 5 personality traits, each with the opposite it is not, and the single feeling the brand must create | card: marketing | needs: positioning, audience | evidence:
- [ ] brief | Creative brief: purpose, audience, personality, mandatories, formats required, constraints, deadline | card: designer | needs: positioning, personality, visual-audit | evidence:
- [ ] review-1 | Independent review of stage 1: verdict and fixes | card: reviewer | needs: positioning, audience, visual-audit, personality, brief | evidence:
## Stage 2: Naming
Gate: one name is chosen, cleared in the target markets, and its handles and domain are available or consciously replaced.
- [ ] name-candidates | At least 20 name candidates in 3 or more distinct approaches, each shortlisted against the brief | card: marketing | needs: brief | evidence:
- [ ] language-check | Shortlisted names checked for meaning, pronunciation and offensive sense in the languages of the target markets | card: researcher | needs: name-candidates | evidence:
- [ ] trademark-check | Trademark search in the registers of the target markets and classes for the shortlist, with conflicts listed and counsel review flagged | card: legal | needs: name-candidates | evidence:
- [ ] handles | Domain and social handle availability for the shortlist, recorded with the date checked | card: marketing | needs: name-candidates | evidence:
- [ ] name | Chosen name with the rationale tied to the positioning and the clearance results | card: marketing | needs: language-check, trademark-check, handles | evidence:
- [ ] review-2 | Independent review of stage 2: verdict and fixes | card: reviewer | needs: name-candidates, language-check, trademark-check, handles, name | evidence:
## Stage 3: Identity
Gate: the identity system works at the smallest size, in one colour, on dark and light, and passes contrast checks.
- [ ] directions | At least 3 distinct logo directions, each as a concept sentence and sketch, with the reason the chosen one won | card: designer | needs: name, brief | evidence:
- [ ] logo | Logo system: primary, mono, small-size versions, clear space | card: designer | needs: directions | evidence:
- [ ] logo-stress | Logo stress test at 16 px, 32 px, one colour, reversed, grayscale and in 3 context mock-ups | card: designer | needs: logo | evidence:
- [ ] colour | Colour palette with exact codes (HEX and RGB, plus print values if print is needed) and measured contrast ratio for every text and background pairing | card: designer | needs: logo | evidence:
- [ ] type | Typography spec: typefaces, hierarchy, sizes, line height, fallbacks, with licence confirmed for web, app and print | card: designer | needs: personality | evidence:
- [ ] similarity-check | Similarity search of the final mark against registered marks and category marks, with counsel review flagged | card: legal | needs: logo | evidence:
- [ ] review-3 | Independent review of stage 3: verdict and fixes | card: reviewer | needs: directions, logo, logo-stress, colour, type, similarity-check | evidence:
## Stage 4: Guidelines and handoff
Gate: someone who did not design the identity can apply it correctly from the files alone.
- [ ] guidelines | Brand guidelines: strategy summary, logo rules, colour, type, imagery, at least 3 mis-use examples, contact for questions | card: designer | needs: logo, colour, type | evidence:
- [ ] voice | Voice and tone guide with do and don't sentence examples and the name's correct spelling and usage | card: marketing | needs: personality, name | evidence:
- [ ] applications | Identity applied to at least 3 real touchpoints (for example site header, social profile, document or slide template) | card: designer | needs: guidelines | evidence:
- [ ] asset-pack | Asset pack: vector source, SVG and PNG exports, favicon and app icon sizes, fonts or licence links, named by a documented convention | card: designer | needs: guidelines | evidence:
- [ ] review-4 | Independent review of stage 4: verdict and fixes | card: reviewer | needs: guidelines, voice, applications, asset-pack | evidence:
